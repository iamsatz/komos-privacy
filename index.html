Kōmos privacy policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026
App: Kōmos (package com.komos.Komos)
Contact: satishkumar.2163@gmail.com

The short version

  • Kōmos has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • Kōmos has no server. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • To show the weather for where you are, the app sends a location (a pair of coordinates, or a place name you typed) straight from your phone to a few public weather and map services listed below. That is all that leaves your phone.
  • Your settings and saved places stay on your phone.

Like any internet request, each of these services can see your phone's internet address when the app contacts it. Kōmos does not control how long these services keep their own logs. Their privacy policies:

  • ipapi.co: https://ipapi.co/privacy/
  • Google (Android location and place names): https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your location

Kōmos asks for location permission once, during setup. You can say yes ("Precise" or "Approximate"), or choose a city instead.

  • Kōmos uses location only while the app is open on your screen. It never asks for location in the background.
  • Your phone's position comes from Google Play services on your phone (the "fused location" service). How Google handles location on your phone is set in your phone's own location settings.
  • The 7 AM morning brief uses your saved Home place (or the last place the app showed), not your live position.
  • Kōmos never sells, shares for advertising, or builds a history of your location. It does not keep a list of where you have been.

You can change location permission at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps → Kōmos → Permissions.

What stays on your phone

Kōmos keeps these on your phone only, in the app's private storage:

  • Your settings: °C or °F, which insights are on, morning brief on or off and its time, who you are (for example "Employee"), whether setup is finished.
  • Your saved places (Home, Work, Favourites) with their coordinates, and the last few cities you chose.
  • The last weather forecast it downloaded, so the app can show something when you are offline.
  • The time you last opened the app, used to time the morning brief.

If your phone's Google backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in your own backup, the same as for other apps. Uninstalling Kōmos, or Settings → Apps → Kōmos → Storage → Clear storage, deletes all of it. Because Kōmos has no server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Kōmos shows a morning brief at 7 AM (or the time you pick) in your Home place's time zone. The brief is written on your phone from the forecast; no message is sent through any Kōmos server.

Children

Kōmos is a general weather app and is not directed at children under 13.

Coming later (not in this version)

Daily routes (the commute between Home and Work) are planned. When they ship, the app will send your start and end points to Google's map and route services to draw the route and check traffic. This policy will be updated, with a new effective date, before that version is released.
